InferSim

Method and assumptions

Operators. Each layer is mapped to GEMMs with (M, N, K) per tensor-parallel rank, plus attention, elementwise work and collectives. FLOPs and bytes give arithmetic intensity; time is the larger of compute (tile-padded FLOPs at achievable peak) and memory traffic (bytes at achievable bandwidth), plus a fixed launch cost. Collectives use an alpha-beta model, taking the faster of ring and one-shot all-reduce.

Architectures. Loaded from Hugging Face config.json: dense GQA (Llama, Qwen), mixture of experts (Mixtral, DeepSeek-V3, with expected active experts per batch), and DeepSeek's multi-head latent attention (weight-absorbed decode over the 576-dim latent). Parameter counts are tested against published sizes.

LPU. All weights live in on-chip SRAM across many chips, executed as a chip-to-chip pipeline. An op larger than one chip's SRAM is split across chips and all-reduced. KV cache must also fit on chip.

Heterogeneous. Disaggregated serving (prefill on GPUs, decode on LPUs, KV cache shipped over a 400 Gb/s link), and an attention-FFN split (attention and KV on GPUs, FFN weights on LPUs, activations crossing twice per layer). Both are labeled speculative.

Validation status. Unit and property tests check invariants (no op beats its roofline, latency is monotonic in batch, Pareto points are non-dominated, calibration recovers known parameters). Calibration against real GPU measurements is the next milestone; until then, treat absolute latencies as estimates and comparisons as the point.
